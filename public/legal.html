Chatwrap is a free tool run by one person. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to WhatsApp or Meta.

Your chats never leave your device

When you connect your WhatsApp folder, your browser reads the files and computes the stats inside the open tab. No message, contact, photo or result is sent to us or to anyone else. Nothing is saved: close the tab and it is gone.

Because we never receive your chats, we cannot see, store, sell or delete them. They stay on your computer, where WhatsApp keeps them.

Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use chat-wrap.app. It follows the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for visitors in the EU, the GDPR. The person responsible is Bastien Schwitz (contact above).

Hosting

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. When you open a page, Vercel's servers process technical data that every website needs: your IP address, the date and time, the page requested, and your browser type. This is used only to deliver the site and keep it secure, and is kept for a short time in Vercel's logs. Vercel may process this data in the USA; it is certified under the Swiss-US and EU-US Data Privacy Frameworks.

No cookies, no tracking profiles

We use no cookies and no advertising. We count anonymous page visits with Vercel Web Analytics: it records which page was opened, the referring website, country, browser and device type, without cookies and without building a profile of you. Your chats and your stats are never part of it. Fonts are served from our own site, not from third parties. If you switch between dark and light mode, your browser remembers that one choice in its local storage. It never leaves your device.

Buy me a coffee

If you choose to buy a coffee, you are sent to a payment page run by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Dublin 2, Ireland. Stripe processes your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive only what Stripe shares with merchants, such as your name, email address and the amount, and use it only for accounting and to answer you if you write to us. We keep it as long as Swiss accounting law requires (10 years). Coffees are voluntary support for a free tool and give no right to a service.

Your rights

You can ask what data about you we hold, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to bschwitz33@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) or, in the EU, to your local data protection authority.
